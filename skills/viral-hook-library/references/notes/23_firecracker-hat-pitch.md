## HOOK (first 1-3s)
Close-up: a hand flicks a lighter and lights a small red FIRECRACKER, sparks flying over grass — then slides the smoking firecracker into an unsuspecting teammate's back jeans pocket. Zero text, zero context. A ticking bomb is planted in second one; you physically cannot scroll before it goes off.

## STRUCTURE
- 0-2s: Lighter + firecracker close-up; fuse sparking.
- 2-4s: The plant: lit firecracker tucked into the back pocket of a guy in a black tee (smoke trailing), who walks off unaware.
- 4-18s: Hard cut to a completely straight merch pitch: teammate in polo stands at a rustic table stacked with gold corduroy "Mountaineers" hats, calmly presenting the hat to camera (turning it, pointing at embroidery) like a QVC segment — while the viewer waits for the pocket bomb.
- 18-20.4s: Pitch continues to the loop point; the implied payoff (bang/reaction) lands at the very end/loop seam, sending you back into the lit-fuse open.

## TEXT OVERLAYS
None at all. No captions, no price, no CTA on screen — all product info lives in the caption. The absence of text is deliberate: nothing competes with the suspense.

## TRANSITIONS & EDIT RHYTHM
Two-scene structure with one hard cut (prank setup → deadpan pitch), then long unbroken takes. The edit trick is TENSION TRANSFER: the open loads a fuse, and the boring-on-purpose pitch runs against that clock (suspense borrowed from Hitchcock's bomb-under-the-table). Loop seam doubles as the punchline.

## AI vs REAL
100% real, single phone camera, outdoors, no graphics. Production cost ~$0 + one firecracker. The craft is comedic structure, not imagery.

## CONCEPT / WHY IT WORKS
Suspense-anchored retention: an unresolved danger planted in the first 2 seconds makes viewers sit through a full 15-second product pitch they'd otherwise skip — watch time and rewatches (loop hides the payoff) explode the algorithmic reach; 85.8K likes for a club team's merch drop. The pitch's deadpan sincerity IS the joke, so the ad reads as content.
