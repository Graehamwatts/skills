## HOOK (first 1-3s)
POV desk shot of an iMac displaying clean typography "we create brands" ("create" in red). A hand reaches up with a paper napkin and physically WIPES THE WORDS OFF THE GLASS mid-read — you're interrupted while reading, which forces a rewatch. The entire video IS the hook; at 7s it's basically a looping magic trick.

## STRUCTURE
- 0-2s: Hand + napkin wipes across the on-screen headline; letters smear/vanish under the cloth as if the pixels were wet ink.
- 3-5s: Reveal: she turns the napkin to camera — the "ink" transferred onto it as handwritten text: "SO YOU CAN OWN YOUR STORY" (OWN in red marker).
- 6-7s: Napkin lowers; screen shows the full intact headline "we create brands" again — seamless loop back to the wipe.

## TEXT OVERLAYS
No overlay layer at all. Two diegetic texts: (1) screen typography — lowercase modern sans, black with the key verb in red; (2) napkin — handwritten caps marker, "OWN" in red. The red accent carries brand color from digital to physical, completing the metaphor.

## TRANSITIONS & EDIT RHYTHM
Single locked-off shot, no cuts visible. The one "transition" is an in-frame effect: a masked screen-content swap timed to the napkin pass (After Effects mask tracks the hand; text erased as it's covered). Loop is engineered so last frame = first frame. Rhythm: one continuous 7s gesture — the anti-edit approach.

## AI vs REAL
Real footage + simple compositing (screen replacement/masked erase). No AI needed; the napkin text is a practical prop. The illusion quality comes from tight hand-tracking on the mask, not generation.

## CONCEPT / WHY IT WORKS
Digital-to-physical metaphor: the brand message literally rubs off the screen into your hand — enacting the tagline (brands that transfer, stories you own). Sub-10s loop means the algorithm reads multiple full watches per viewer; the trick demands a second watch to see HOW. Message + mechanism are the same thing, which is why it feels smart rather than gimmicky.

## REPLICATE FOR A LISTING
Graeham wipes his laptop screen showing "Just Listed: [address]" — the words transfer onto a napkin that flips to reveal "SOLD in 9 days" or "Your equity, your story"; or wipe a Zillow listing off the screen onto a coffee-shop napkin: "Stop scrolling. Start touring." Requires one AE screen-replacement mask (or hire an editor for ~$50). Difficulty 3/5 — trivial to film, moderate to composite cleanly.
