# Ad specs, compliance and the log

Platform limits below were checked in October 2026. Platforms change them, so confirm in the ad preview before you write more than a draft.

## Naming

Name every ad `<street and number> | <platform> | <angle letter and version> | <start date>`. The letters match the creative files (A, B, C, D1, D2 and so on), so the seller report and the platforms agree.

## Graeham's calls for each listing (ask, do not assume)

- Which angles start, the budget total, and the end date.
- Price on the creative. His standing rule keeps price out of listing videos, but he asked for price hooks on the first run, so offer one price version and one without.
- Where the click goes: the property page with its tour form (used on the first run), or an Instant Form. The standing Instant Form setup is in `C:\Users\Graeham Watts\Documents\Assistants\Team Directory and roles & tasks\Director of Ads & Web - Role Definition.md`: 15 mile radius, "More Volume" form, a custom timeline question, prefilled name, email and phone, privacy link graehamwatts.com/site/privacy-terms.
- Whether to run Search at all when volume is thin.
- Where a teaser sends people when there is no property page yet.

## Facebook and Instagram

Use the meta-ads skill for every call. It holds the confirmation gate and the tool notes. Meta applies the Housing category by itself when it detects a housing ad, and you set it at creation anyway. Targeting is the place only: a radius of 15 miles or more around the listing, age and gender fixed, no ZIP, lookalike or saved audiences. Use one lifetime budget shared across the ads in the set, with a start and an end, so the schedule ends on its own. Run two ads first and keep the rest in reserve. Compare angle against angle with the same body.

Formats: 1:1 or 4:5 images for feeds, 9:16 for Reels and Stories with the bottom third kept clear of text. Primary text and headline truncate on some placements, so put the claim in the first line and read the preview. Call to action Learn More, destination the property page. Tag the URL with utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign (internal only, never printed in a seller document).

## YouTube

Skippable in stream through Google Ads, as a Video reach campaign or Demand Gen, whichever the account offers (Google moved Video action campaigns into Demand Gen by April 2026). Viewers can skip after 5 seconds, so the real city, the hook and the offer all land in the first 5. Horizontal 16:9 at 1920 x 1080. A 15 second cut is enough to start, and a 30 second cut that shares its body can follow, so only the opening differs. Upload to the real channel, @graehamwatts (hundreds of videos), not the similarly named empty @graehamwatts2709, and only after Graeham says yes, because an upload publishes a file to his channel. The Google Ads account has to be linked to that channel. Google may hold a new advertiser for verification, as it did on the first run, so start that early and treat the switch on date as unknown until it clears. Billing and verification are Graeham's.

## Google Search

Responsive search ads take up to 15 headlines of 30 characters, up to 4 descriptions of 90, and two path fields of 15. Pin only what must stay fixed. Build two or three ad groups by intent (the listing's own terms, the address and community, the nearby bigger market) with phrase and exact match, the negatives from research.md, and location by city or radius, never ZIP. Clicks go to the property page. When the listing's own terms show little or no volume, say so in the plan and ask whether to run Search at all. Prepared copy can wait.

## Other channels

TikTok needs its account region fixed before anything runs. Google Display is a later option and is moving into Demand Gen. ChatGPT ads have their own skill. Google Business Profile posts and organic posting go to Ellie.

## Setting up paused

Meta: create paused through meta-ads. Google: not every flow has a paused on create switch, so set the start date days ahead before you publish, pause the campaign immediately, and confirm the status reads Paused before you leave the page. Never leave one Scheduled or Enabled, because a scheduled start spends without anyone switching it on. If you cannot confirm the status, say so and do not claim it is paused. Read every setup back from the platform: name, budget total, schedule, audience, destination URL, ad text.

## The landing page and tracking

Before spend, open the property page and check for Box links (use the Drive link instead), a retired tagline or any em dash, REALTOR without the registered mark, stale price or status, and that the originals of any altered image can be reached. Report what you find. Edits to the GHL page are done by hand (Adrian or Ellie), because the page builder cannot be driven by automation. Confirm the tour form reaches GHL and that the pixel and GA4 fire. A test lead can notify the team, so ask Graeham first and use a clearly labeled test contact.

## Compliance checklist

Run it before Peter gets the brief and again before setup.

- [ ] MLS entry live, or a neighborhood teaser with no address or price
- [ ] Housing category set on Meta, no restricted targeting on Google
- [ ] Real city in the headline and the first five seconds, and nothing implies a different address
- [ ] No claims about schools, safety, family fit, walkability or travel time, and no financing terms
- [ ] Every number dated and sourced, comparison wording says "price", pending comparisons marked and not released
- [ ] Graeham Watts, Compass and the DRE from identity.json present, REALTOR with the mark and not beside a place
- [ ] Altered images carry the statement and a link or QR code to the original, and which images are altered comes from the production notes or Peter's delivery note, not from a guess
- [ ] Every video was actually looked at (frames at 0, 3 and 5 seconds if you cannot play it), or this check is marked open
- [ ] Price on the creative is a decision Graeham made for this listing
- [ ] No dash characters in the copy, humanizer pass done
- [ ] Music commercially licensed, captions readable on a phone
