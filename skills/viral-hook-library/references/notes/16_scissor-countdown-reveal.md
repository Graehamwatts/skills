## TEXT OVERLAYS
Zero digital overlays. All text is PHYSICAL: hand-lettered black script on red paper (three escalating urgency lines), then the phone's own screen (countdown, promo code) as the final "text". The handwriting doubles as brand typography.

## TRANSITIONS & EDIT RHYTHM
One locked-off overhead shot; the "transitions" are physical layer-cuts — each scissor pass functions as a wipe that reveals the next message (a practical mask wipe). Probably 2-3 hidden cuts to swap paper layers, invisible because framing never moves. Pacing is slow and satisfying (craft-ASMR rhythm), urgency delivered by copy, not cut speed.

## AI vs REAL
Entirely practical: paper, scissors, marble, phone with the live site open. The countdown on-screen is just their real website widget. No AI, no motion graphics — the analog texture IS the differentiator.

## CONCEPT / WHY IT WORKS
Escalating-urgency striptease: three layers of copy (Last chance → Don't miss it → Final day) mirror a countdown funnel, and the physical cutting makes urgency feel tangible. Craft satisfaction earns full watch time; comment-gate ("Comment SALE") plus the massive objection-handling caption (price = investment, client testimonials) does the actual selling below the fold.
