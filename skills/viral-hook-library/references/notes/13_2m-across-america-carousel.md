## TRANSITIONS & EDIT RHYTHM
Carousel, so "transitions" are swipes; the template consistency does the work — same layout every slide means your eye only registers what changed (city + price). Cover slide's drawn arrow explicitly prompts the swipe. Each embedded clip is a single slow gimbal/drone shot, no cutting inside slides.

## AI vs REAL
Real listing/tour footage from four different agents' markets. Zero AI. The production trick is COLLABORATION: four agents each contribute one clip, and the collab post publishes to multiple audiences simultaneously.

## CONCEPT / WHY IT WORKS
Price-anchoring comparison content ("what $X gets you in different cities") is evergreen and inherently debate-generating — the caption explicitly farms comments ("Which market are you picking?") and shares ("Share this with someone"). The multi-agent collab multiplies reach and borrows credibility across markets; each agent's headshot bubble makes attribution (and lead routing) explicit.
