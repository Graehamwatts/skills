## CONCEPT / WHY IT WORKS
Impossible-object pattern interrupt: keys physically flying to the listing creates instant "what am I watching" curiosity, while grounding shots in the real, recognizable town builds local relevance. The AI hook earns 3 seconds of attention that the ordinary listing tour then converts. The keys are also a perfect metaphor (keys = new home) so the gimmick is on-message.
