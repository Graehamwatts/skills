# Viral Hook Library — how we open and cut every video

This skill exists because Graeham hand-picked 29 reference videos (July 2026) and said:
"replicate them — especially the hooks, and the smooth beat-synced transitions. All our
future videos should feel like these."

## How to use

1. Read `references/style-playbook.md` FIRST — it is the ruleset: 8 hook families,
   transition grammar, beat-sync method, text systems, AI/real blend rules, engagement
   engineering, production tiers, and the standing 7-step formula.
2. Pick the hook family for the video at hand (H1-H8). The hook is chosen BEFORE the script.
3. If the concept needs AI generation, hand the chosen family to `cinematic-hooks` for
   prompt writing, and `viral-video-engine` for production. Beat-sync method: detect the
   music's beat grid (librosa), snap every cut and canvas move to it, hook arrival on a
   downbeat, alternate 1-beat and 2/4-beat shots.
4. For a specific reference ("do the flying keys one", "the trunk skit"), read its note in
   `references/notes/` — every note has HOOK / STRUCTURE / TRANSITIONS / REPLICATE FOR A
   LISTING sections with a difficulty score.
5. Deliver two masters per the playbook: music-baked beat-synced + mute-cut (for in-app
   trending audio). Commercial tracks are NEVER baked — in-app only.

## Hard rules (from the playbook — enforced every build)

- GRAEHAM DOES NOT APPEAR ON CAMERA — ever. All human presence is AI: Nano Banana Pro composite
  (Graeham-RealPhotos element + anti-cleft block, signature outfit: white oxford sleeves rolled +
  navy chinos), Seedance animation, HeyGen photo-avatar + voice clone for speech. Property footage
  stays real.

- Every video opens with ONE of the 8 hook families. No hook, no build.
- AI generates the impossible 3 seconds; real footage sells the house; continuity hides the seam.
- Every cut lands on the beat grid when music-driven; on sentence boundaries when VO-driven.
- One text system per video. Diegetic text beats overlays.
- Loop-engineer the ending; comment-gate one asset; the caption carries the specs.
