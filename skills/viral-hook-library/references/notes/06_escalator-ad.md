## CONCEPT / WHY IT WORKS
A physical metaphor rendered literally: same buyer, same destination, two experiences — effort vs ease. Clone-twin effects reliably cause a double-take ("wait, is that the same person?"), and the labels resolve the joke instantly into a sales message. The caption ("Which one are you going to choose?") converts the metaphor into a CTA.
