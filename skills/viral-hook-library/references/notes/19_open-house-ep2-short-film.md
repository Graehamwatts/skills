## TEXT OVERLAYS
Subtitles: orange/amber ITALIC serif, sentence case, bottom-center — film-subtitle styling, not IG captions. Chapter cards ("The Sauna", "The Kitchen") in cream serif, top-center. End card in ornate script on green — behaves like movie titles, zero platform-native text.

## TRANSITIONS & EDIT RHYTHM
All hard cuts, edited like an A24 comedy: shot/reverse-shot for dialogue, one drone establisher, macro inserts (pool ball) as scene punctuation, chapter cards as act breaks, one BTS 4th-wall cut for a laugh. Pacing is conversational (3-6s shots), not beat-driven — retention comes from story, and the loop restarts as episodic cliffhanger.

## AI vs REAL
100% real: real actors/agents, real homeowner character, real estate, real film crew (shown on camera). Film emulation (grain, halation, warm LUT) does the "expensive" work. No AI anywhere.

## CONCEPT / WHY IT WORKS
A "Coming Soon" listing announcement disguised as a mockumentary sitcom: the seller who doesn't want to sell is a genius inversion — it dramatizes demand ("We sold our last four") while making the team the likable protagonists. Narrative transportation + episodic structure (EP. 2, date tease) converts a one-off listing into a series audiences subscribe to.
