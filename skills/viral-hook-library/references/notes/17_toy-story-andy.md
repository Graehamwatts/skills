## HOOK (first 1-3s)
Empty luxury home, floating stair. A woman in a blue floral dress ("Andy") sprints INTO frame arms flailing while giant white caps "ANDY" slam on at bottom of screen. Static top text (persists whole video): "POV: eres asesor inmobiliario pero ahí viene Andy" (POV: you're a real estate agent but here comes Andy). Toy Story premise is instantly legible: the agents are the toys.

## STRUCTURE

- 2-4s: Cut: male agent standing perfectly still in kitchen holding phone — frozen "toy pose."
- 4-6s: He leaps ONTO the kitchen island mid-air like a tossed action figure ("ANDY!").
- 6-8s: Andy runs deeper into the house; cut to stairs — the woman is now a literal DOLL lying on a step (miniaturized figure).

- 10-13s: Empty bedroom — woman crouched on floor toy-style, then springs up, hair whipping (played in reverse or jump-cut revival).
- 13-16s: Walk-in closet: male agent mid-freeze dance ("AHÍ"), Wheezy-style penguin toy sitting on a shelf; he ends seated rigidly in the display nook like a doll on a shelf ("¡AHÍ").
- 16-19s: Buzz-body agent lying limp on the closet shelf; loop restarts at kitchen.

## TEXT OVERLAYS
Two layers: (1) persistent hook line, top-center, white bold condensed sans, 2 lines, subtle shadow, never moves; (2) karaoke captions — single giant words ("ANDY", "AHÍ") bottom-center, white extra-bold caps with heavy drop shadow, popping per beat of the audio line.

## TRANSITIONS & EDIT RHYTHM
All hard cuts, one per beat/word of the audio — room-to-room jump cuts sell the "she's getting closer" chase. Two effect shots inside the cuts: the doll-on-stairs (scale composite/prop swap) and Buzz Lightyear body with real face (AI face-swap or After Effects composite) dropped in as straight cuts, no morphs. One apparent reverse/speed-ramp when the crouched woman springs back to life. Rhythm is fast (~1.5-2s per shot), comedic timing over musicality.

## AI vs REAL
90% real handheld footage in an actual vacant listing. The Buzz Lightyear shot is AI/composite (agent's real face on toy body); the stair doll is either a real doll prop or a scale composite. Blend is intentionally imperfect — the uncanny cut IS the joke.

## CONCEPT / WHY IT WORKS
Meme-franchise mapping: Toy Story's "freeze when Andy comes" rule recast as agents scrambling when a buyer shows up — a POV joke every viewer decodes in one second. The listing gets a full walkthrough (living, kitchen, stairs, bedroom, walk-in closet) smuggled inside a skit, so it never feels like an ad. Humor + persistent POV text = rewatches and shares among agents AND buyers.
