The real "transition" device is the MATCH ON LOGO: every shot re-centers the same wordmark at similar scale/position, so cuts feel like one object teleporting across contexts — a serial match cut.

## AI vs REAL

Consistency of the wordmark across surfaces is the AI showpiece. Lighting/texture quality is high; motion within shots is minimal (near-still cinemagraph feel), typical of image-to-video.
