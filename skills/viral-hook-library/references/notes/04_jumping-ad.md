## TRANSITIONS & EDIT RHYTHM
Essentially zero cuts — one locked-off wide shot. All rhythm comes from the dancer's moves and the text animation, which appear beat-synced (text state changes ~every 3-4s, dance accents faster). Signature: static camera + kinetic subject + kinetic typography.

## AI vs REAL
100% real footage — a realtor literally dancing in his empty listing with graphics added in post. No AI anywhere. The production cost is one tripod shot plus typography.

## CONCEPT / WHY IT WORKS
Incongruity humor + confidence display: nobody expects a "serious luxury broker" to dance alone in an empty flat, and the deadpan billboard text plays it straight, which makes it funnier and extremely memorable. It also shows the product (spacious empty apartment) the entire time, and the caption pivots hard into a serious trust pitch, creating a likable contrast.
