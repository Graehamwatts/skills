# Hook & CTA Library — Watts brand voice (dry, data-first, no hype)

## Hooks (fill-in templates)

### Price anchor (default Reel 1)
- "This is what $[PRICE] buys on the Peninsula right now — and most buyers will never even see it."
- "Everyone says you can't buy in [CITY] under $[X]. This house says otherwise."
- "$[PRICE]. [CITY]. Not a typo."

### Price-guess engagement
- "How much do you think this [CITY] house costs? DM me your guess — price at the end."
- (Overlay on hook frame: "GUESS THE PRICE 👇 answer at the end")

### Contrarian / lot-first (Reel 2)
- "The house isn't the story here. The land is."
- "The cheapest house on the street is the most valuable one. Here's why."

### Flaw-forward honesty
- "This $[PRICE] listing has one problem nobody's mentioning. I'll show you."
- "Let's be honest about what this is: [FLAW]. Now let me show you why it doesn't matter."

### Story cold-open (Glennda pattern — deal recaps)
- "My buyer offered $[X] under asking. The seller said yes in four hours. Here's what actually happened."
- "This house sat for [N] days. One change — gone in a weekend."

### Local specificity boosters (weave into any hook)
Ten minutes from Stanford / across the Dumbarton from Meta / [N] minutes to [EMPLOYER].

## Hook rules
No greetings. No name/logo before ~20s. Number or negation in the first sentence.
Pair the verbal hook with a visual pattern-interrupt (walking shot, drone reveal, unexpected first frame).
The hook is a promise — pay it off or lose the follow.

## CTAs (one per video — say it, show it, pin it)

### Listing (default)
Spoken: "DM me '[KEYWORD]' for the full details and private tour times — or call me directly, number's in my bio."
Overlay (last 3-5s): DM "[KEYWORD]" · OR CALL — LINK IN BIO
Pinned comment: "[KEYWORD]" gets full details + disclosures + tour link. Or call/text me: [PHONE].

### Price-guess
"DM me your guess — I'll tell you how close you were, and what it'd actually take to get the keys."

### Market update
"DM me 'MARKET' and I'll send the full [CITY] report for your zip."

### YouTube long-form
Verbal CTA + description first 2 lines (phone + DM instruction) + pinned comment.
"Comment '[KEYWORD]' below or DM me on Instagram @[HANDLE] — and if you're serious, just call me. Number's in the description."

### Keyword conventions
Listing keyword = street name (BRADLEY). Keep GHL comment-keywords (EPA, SELL, BUY, MARKET...) wired for automation; DM/call is the high-intent lane. Speed-to-lead target: reply < 5 minutes.

## Caption grammar (burned-in)
3-5 words per line, max 2 lines, Inter semi-bold on dark pill, 1-3 keyword highlights (gold #E3C567) per caption — nouns/verbs only.

## v4 trailer-hook recipe (client-locked, July 2026)
Frame 1: giant gradient-gold price SLAM (`el_gradient_headline`, 'slam') over the first of 4-6
rapid cuts (fireplace/kitchen/exterior/aerial/walk-mid-stride, 0.5-0.9s each). The number hits
before a single word lands. Payoff line (two-tone white+gold, e.g. "MOST BUYERS NEVER SEE IT.")
slides in at bottom-third as the hook sentence ends. Price exits before the avatar's face carries
a beat. This replaced a single walking-shot opener that tested as "the videos you swipe over."
