## HOOK (first 1-3s)
Clean-cut guy in a striped polo stands in a warm wood-cabinet kitchen holding a gray coffee mug, deadpan to camera. Word-by-word white caption at chest height; opening line reveals "...in the cup." — i.e. there is NO coffee in the cup. The hook is a spoken confession that the video itself is fake, delivered while he tilts the visibly empty mug at the lens. Curiosity gap: "wait, what's fake?"

## STRUCTURE
- 0-2s: "You're probably [wondering]... there's no coffee in the cup" — tilts empty mug to camera.
- 3-6s: "but... you're... " builds the bit; holds mug, deadpan face.
- 7-10s: Raises phone showing a voice-memo waveform: "the voice speaking to you? prerecorded" — mouth not synced, proving the gag.
- 11-14s: Holds up a bagel: "this bagel... fake." Bites it anyway on a later beat (physical-comedy button).
- 15-17s: "Not even mine... everything here is fake... except this house." — thrusts phone/hand at lens, glances around.
- 18-25s: Hard pivot to listing montage: kitchen push-in, living room glide, freestanding-tub bathroom; three stacked black text pills persist: "Barton Hills 📍 / 5 Bed | 4 Bath / Heated Pool & Spa". Loops back to mug confession.

## TEXT OVERLAYS
Talking half: white sans-serif word-by-word karaoke captions with black outline, lower-center, 1-3 words per frame. Tour half: three rounded BLACK pill overlays with white title-case text stacked top-center (location pin emoji on line 1), static — no animation, pinned across every tour shot.

## TRANSITIONS & EDIT RHYTHM
All HARD CUTS. Talking segment = jump cuts on the same locked framing, each cut swapping the prop in his hands (mug → phone → bagel) — the prop swap IS the transition gag. One hard smash cut from face to tour montage at the reveal line ("except this house"), a deliberate tonal snap. Tour shots are slow gimbal push-ins, ~2-3s each, evenly paced rather than beat-slammed.

## AI vs REAL
100% real footage. The joke is ABOUT fakery (empty cup, prerecorded VO, prop bagel) but it's all practical, shot in the actual listing's kitchen. The listing montage is standard pro gimbal real-estate footage. No AI anywhere — the meta-honesty is the differentiator in an AI-saturated feed.

## CONCEPT / WHY IT WORKS
Radical-honesty pattern interrupt: confessing the artifice of content marketing ("everything here is fake") buys enormous trust, so the single claimed truth — "except this house" — lands with full force. It also weaponizes curiosity (viewers stay to hear WHAT is fake next) and the punchline structurally forces the listing reveal. 5,417 shares says the bit travels beyond the listing audience.
