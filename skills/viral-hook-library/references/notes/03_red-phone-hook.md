## HOOK (first 1-3s)
A bright RED vintage rotary telephone sits alone in the middle of an empty multi-lane city street, dwarfed by teal-glass skyscrapers (Dubai-style skyline, VOCO tower) under moody gray sky. Ultra-wide low-angle lens makes the tiny phone vs giant city scale contrast pop. No text on screen. The absurd object placement IS the hook: why is a landline in the road?

## STRUCTURE
- 0-2s: Static/slow low-angle wide — red phone alone on the asphalt, city towering behind.
- ~3-5s: A man (white tee, denim shorts, sneakers) enters and crouches down, reaching for the receiver — fisheye-ish perspective, his hand motion-blurred.
- ~6-9s: He holds the receiver to his ear, crouched, looking off pensively at the skyline; coiled cord stretches down to the phone. Held beat, near-still.
- ~10-12s: Cut back to the phone alone on the street (bookend), then loop restarts.

## TRANSITIONS & EDIT RHYTHM
Very few cuts — essentially 3 shots with long holds; possibly one slow-motion take split up. The stillness itself is the rhythm: no fast cuts, letting the surreal image breathe. Muted teal-and-orange colour grade (desaturated city, saturated red phone) is the signature effect — the phone is the only warm object in frame.

## AI vs REAL
Looks like REAL footage (or extremely clean production): consistent shadows, real lens distortion, believable fabric/skin. Likely a real prop phone placed on a quiet street with a color grade. Nothing reads as AI-generated. It proves the same pattern interrupt works with a $20 thrift-store prop and no AI.

## CONCEPT / WHY IT WORKS
Single-prop surrealism plus a curiosity gap: an anachronistic object (rotary phone) in an impossible place makes the brain stop to resolve the incongruity, and answering it implies "someone is calling YOU" — perfect teaser energy for a "coming soon" reveal. Color isolation (one red object in a desaturated frame) directs the eye instantly.
