That's exactly what a small pistachio-focused bakery in Miami's Wynwood district did by using AI-generated storytelling to create a viral video that looked like a dramatic high-speed police chase..."

## HOOK (first 1-3s)
Fake BREAKING NEWS: CCTV overhead of a white Corvette weaving through stopped traffic at a rail crossing, full local-news dressing — red "PDN 9 MIAMI" chyron "HIGH SPEED CHASE LEADS TO RESTAURANT", weather ticker "73°|84°", timestamp "12-15-2025". Static repost overlay on black: "Now this is how you use AI to market your business 😂" (white sans, "use AI"/"market your business" in yellow). News-format hijack = instant credibility + curiosity.

## STRUCTURE
- 0-13s: CCTV chase montage: Corvette dodges rail-crossing barriers, train passes, two police cruisers blocked.
- 14-18s: Cut: storefront of "Pistacchio — pane e caffè" (green pistachio logo); the chase "destination" revealed.
- 19-28s: More CCTV: hooded suspect sprints down a wet sidewalk, cop chase continues on foot; red news bumper + mugshot/height-chart graphic of the "suspect".
- 29-38s: Interior b-roll of the bakery: pendant lights, logo wall, pastry case labeled "Pan al Pistacchio / Cornetto Crema / Croissant al Pistacchio" — full menu tour disguised as evidence footage.
- 39-49s: Suspect "caught" outside the café; stands sheepishly under the Pistacchio signage.
- 50-55s: Hero shot: pistachio-shaped dessert in a takeout box (the MacGuffin — he was racing to get it before close).
- 56-63s: Two officers "arrest" him inside the bakery; closing witness-interview talking head outside. Loop.

## TEXT OVERLAYS
Persistent black letterbox top/bottom with the repost commentary line (white/yellow sans). Inside the video: broadcast graphics package — red lower-third chyron, station bug "PDN 9", weather, timestamps, mugshot graphic. All text is diegetic news UI; that's the disguise.

## TRANSITIONS & EDIT RHYTHM
News-edit grammar: hard cuts between "camera sources" (CCTV high angle, bodycam-ish handheld, b-roll, interview) plus one red channel-bumper wipe (station rebrand graphic) mid-video. Cut pace ~4-5s, documentary rhythm — believability comes from imitating broadcast pacing, not beat-sync.

## AI vs REAL
Entire "news footage" is AI-generated (Veo/Seedance-class): chase, train, cops, suspect, even the bakery b-roll — consistent grade and camera artifacts sell it. The only real thing is the brand (real Wynwood bakery). Repost frame (black bars + commentary) is standard editing.

## CONCEPT / WHY IT WORKS
Format hijack: it wears the costume of breaking news, so the brain grants it attention and trust before realizing it's an ad ("first you grab attention, then you reveal the brand" — the caption spells it out). The absurd payoff (chase → pistachio croissant) converts deception into a joke, so viewers share instead of feeling tricked. Story first, brand as punchline.
