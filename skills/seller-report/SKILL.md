## What this is and who reads it

A weekly letter to the seller of one listing, in Graeham's name. His words: "put together a fully detailed seller report to show the seller that we are doing our job and following up with people and showing them what that feedback is... So it's a lot easier for me when I need to come back and say, Hey, we need a price reduction, look at all the seller reports, this is what everyone's saying."

The reader is a homeowner on a phone, and the draft does go to them. Each issue is numbered, dated a Monday, and covers the Monday to Sunday week that just ended. Top to bottom: a short brief, one numbers table (this week, last week, since we listed), the weekly charts, "What people are saying" with price as plain counts; then "The details", grouped by what people did (offers and likely offers, agents who showed the home, the disclosure package list, open houses, other inquiries), this week's people in full and earlier people one line each; then the optional sections, next week, and the running record since listing. The look is his CMA system: black #1A1A1A, gold #C5A55A, cream #F5EFDC, Montserrat and Inter. `references/report-structure.md` says what each part is for.

One build writes, into `issues\NN\`: `email.html` (the report), `email_gmail.html` (the same words, made safe for the Gmail connector: this is the one that goes into the draft), `link.html` (the forwardable copy with no offer details), `print.html` and `report.pdf`, and phone pictures in `previews\`.

## What the operator does: three steps

1. Hand over the information or say where it is ("run the weekly for Sample Glen, the sheet is up to date").
2. Get the finished report as an **unsent Gmail draft**, To the seller, CC Adrian.
3. Read it like any draft and say in plain words what to change ("Jordan never said it was overpriced", "take out the part about the neighbour"). Claude applies it, rebuilds and replaces the draft body. The correction is saved and holds in every later week.

That is the whole experience. No review table, no confirm step, no sign-off, and nobody is asked to look over how the notes were read. Graeham saw a version with a review step and said: "Don't know what it is or what it does, but I don't know why we need it. This is not what I had in mind." Keep workshop words (tag, tagging, curation, ledger, provenance, validation) out of the report, the operator guide and every chat reply.

## The rules, and why each exists

1. **Draft, never send. One clean build.** It speaks to his client in his name, so Graeham sends it himself. Every build is the finished report: no notice on top, no panel at the end, nothing addressed to anyone but the seller, so no working material can ever go out.
2. **Feedback only.** His words: "The system should only provide feedback." Never recommend a price change, never suggest a number, never say what silence on price means. He makes the pricing case in person with the stack of reports as evidence; a report that argues for him reads as pressure and weakens that evidence.
3. **Tone.** His words: "Friendly, but concise and direct, but should be nuanced to also not offend people... We don't want to be quoting those kinds of terrible things about someone's home. We can just say they provided negative feedback and specifically said that the pricing was too high." The substance stays, the sting goes, every comment is the team's paraphrase "as reported to our team", and nothing is in quote marks. Claude's own sentences (readings, recaps, next week, a brief file) also follow `cma-generator/references/report-build.md` Part 3: no opener that braces the reader, no "no agenda" or "no-pressure" phrasing, no apology for the data source or the tools. The tone check fails a page that has one.
4. **Privacy.** No phone numbers, emails, lockbox or door codes, buyer health, family or finances, first-person asides from the notes, or the address of any other listing outside the similar homes section. Agents print as "First L." with brokerage. Sellers forward these, and the people in them never agreed to be in them.
5. **Real data only.** No invented numbers, names, quotes or dates; "not recorded" where a fact is missing. Another AI once put invented ad results in a seller report and it could not be sent. One made-up number makes the whole stack worthless as evidence.
6. **Only when asked, only what was asked for.** Nothing runs on a schedule. Read only sources the operator handed over or pointed to in this session. The extras (online views, similar homes, ads, time on the market, photo) are occasional: "we might not do it all week, every week, every time until we have our PropertyIQ system up and running." In the code: online views and similar homes print only from a reading taken for this issue's week; time on the market prints whenever `market.json` is on file and the ads section while a channel is on, so on a week they were not asked for pass `--no-market` and `--no-marketing` (step 6).
7. **One way to count.** Every number is computed in code by `references/counting-rules.md` and recounted by the checks. Never type or adjust a number. A hand-built report once miscounted empty open houses and double counted agents with their own buyers.
8. **Brand from identity.json.** Name, REALTOR with the registered mark, brokerage line, DRE, phone, email and the Compass disclaimer come from `skills/shared-references/identity.json` at run time. Never write a blocklisted value from that file anywhere: a wrong DRE has leaked into client work before.
9. **No client data in the skills repo.** It syncs to GitHub. Real data lives only in `C:\Users\Graeham Watts\Documents\Skills LLMS\Claude\reports\seller-reports\<slug>\`; the sheet export, which holds phone numbers, goes in the session scratchpad and never in the listing's report folder (`references/intake.md`, section 3). Nothing is written inside `Documents\Listings` unless Graeham asks for a final copy filed there. Tests use invented people.
10. **Doubt goes in the chat reply, never in the report.** At most five plain lines under "Worth a glance before you send". The checks run silently; mention one only when it fails and you cannot fix it.

## Workflow

`<scripts>` is `C:\Users\Graeham Watts\Documents\Skills LLMS\Claude\Skills\skills\seller-report\scripts`, `<listing>` is the listing's folder under `reports\seller-reports\`, `<scratch>` is the session scratchpad. Quote every path. Needs Python 3 with openpyxl and Pillow, and Microsoft Edge for the PDF, the phone pictures and the layout check.

1. **Pick the listing.** One listing, one folder, one draft per run. With no `<listing>\listing.json`, set the listing up first (`references/intake.md`, section 2). Under contract or pending: ask whether a report is still wanted.
2. **Get the live sheet** through the Drive connector: `search_files` for `title contains 'View Activity' and title contains '<street>'` (skip it when `listing.json` has `sheet.drive_id`), then `download_file_content` with `exportMimeType` `application/vnd.openxmlformats-officedocument.spreadsheetml.sheet`, and decode the saved result into `<scratch>` (the one-line decoder is in `references/intake.md`). Never `read_file_content`: it cuts the long feedback cells short.
3. **Numbering and the cross-check.** `python "<scripts>\snapshot_issue.py" next --folder "<listing>"` gives the number. When `listing.json` has `numbering.first_issue_number` and an issue is on file, the number is settled and Gmail is not searched. On a listing's first issue under this skill, or when the number is in doubt, ask how many reports the seller has had and, with the operator's go-ahead, search Gmail for Adrian's weekly status emails for this address: count what actually reached the seller (sent to them, not drafts), and the series continues from that. The same search shows whether a hand-written report for this same week already went out; if so, say it in a glance line. When pointed to this week's status email, its counts are a cross-check, never a source (`references/intake.md`, sections 3 and 7).
4. **Run the issue.**
   ```
   python "<scripts>\run_issue.py" --folder "<listing>" --sheet "<scratch>\<slug>-view-activity.xlsx" --as-of <export day>
   ```
   Add only what applies: `--source "status_email|The team's weekly status email|<its date>"` once per extra source handed over this session, `--glance "<a plain line>"` for your own lines, `--report-date <that Monday>` when built late. On a listing's first issue under this skill, add one `--glance` line with the totals of the last hand-written report the seller received and this report's totals, and why they differ (no code writes it; `references/intake.md`, section 7). `--help` lists the rest. Each stop prints one line starting `NEXT:` with the exact command to run again; use it, because it keeps your switches. Exit codes: 0 READY; 2 an input is missing; 3 that issue was already sent (ask before `--replace-sent`); 10 notes to read (step 5); 11 the numbers could not be built (place the row or give the date it names, never adjust a count); 20 a page did not render (a data or code fault, fix what is named); 30 a check failed (nothing was kept; fix the wording with step 11 and rebuild).
5. **Read the new notes** (exit 10). `<listing>\work\todo.json` lists only what is new. Write the seller-safe wording and what each note says into a readings file in `<listing>\work\` by `references/reading-and-tone.md`, put the open house recaps in plain words in the same pass, then:
   ```
   python "<scripts>\curation_tools.py" apply --folder "<listing>" --readings "<listing>\work\readings.json"
   ```
   and run the NEXT line again. This reading is silent: never show it or ask anyone to confirm it.
6. **Extras, only when asked this session** (or when `listing.json` `extras.<source>.every_issue` is true), before the final run: online views (`online_views.py`: Zillow, one page, views and saves since listing), similar homes nearby (`nearby_update.py plan --asked`; the site follows the watch list, so a list chosen from the MLS is planned on MLSListings, where Graeham signs in himself and `nearby_from_mls.py` reads the grids), the photo (`hero_photo.py set`, so it stays in `listing.json`), the ads status (`marketing.json`), time on the market (`market.json`). One lookup each, a block is a stop. Commands and rules: `references/extras.md` and `references/market-and-marketing.md`. Then run the NEXT line again. **What prints without being asked:** online views and similar homes print only from this week's reading, but time on the market prints whenever `market.json` is on file and the ads section while a channel is on. On a week the operator did not ask for them, add `--no-market` and `--no-marketing` to the run line; `--no-online` and `--no-nearby` leave out a reading that was taken but is not wanted. Keep the same switches on every rebuild of that issue.
7. **Look at it yourself** when it says READY. Read the phone pictures in `<listing>\issues\NN\previews\` (`email_phone_01.png` onward: the first three tiles and anything that changed). Check the address, the greeting and that every name belongs to this listing. Fix a sentence that reads badly now (step 11).
8. **Find the draft that may already be there.** Look at `issues\NN\manifest.json` `delivery.gmail_draft_id`; if it is empty, `list_drafts` with the query `subject:"Weekly Seller Report No. NN" "<street name>"`. A draft found either way is this issue's draft: replace its body with `update_draft` (`draftId`, `htmlBody` only) instead of making a second one, then check and record it as below.
9. **Make the draft** from `issues\NN\email_gmail.html`, never `email.html`. The Gmail connector removes every image, turns call links into dead links, strips background styles, the head, style blocks and class and data attributes, and turns web links and plain-text domains into redirects; the Gmail-safe copy is drawn for exactly that. `references/delivery.md` has the full recipe; in short: `create_draft` with `to` the seller, `cc` graehamwattsclientcare@gmail.com, the `subject` copied from `"subject"` in `issues\NN\manifest.json` (`Weekly Seller Report No. NN · <Month D, YYYY> - <address>`; never from the console, which can garble the middle dot), and `htmlBody` the file's text copied exactly (70 to 95 KB: read it in line ranges and join them with nothing added or dropped). Then `get_draft` with `messageFormat` RAW, and on the file the harness saves:
   ```
   python "<scripts>\draft_check.py" --issue "<listing>\issues\NN" --draft "<saved get_draft result>"
   python "<scripts>\snapshot_issue.py" record --folder "<listing>" --issue <N> --gmail-draft-id <id>
   ```
   `draft_check` fails on an image, a dead link, a web link the connector made (a domain auto-linked into a google.com redirect), different words, a wrong subject, a To with nobody but the team, or a CC that is not Adrian; a street suffix written out (Ct against Court) is only a note. If an `update_draft` (to add the CC, or to replace the body) is refused, tell Graeham in one plain line what the draft still needs and never work around it. Ask for the seller's address if you do not have it; never store it in a file.
10. **Reply in chat**, short: which report, that it is in drafts unsent, To and CC, then "Worth a glance before you send" with the lines from `<listing>\notes_for_operator.json` `worth_a_glance` (five at most, most important first; leave the heading out when empty), then an invitation to say what to change. No list of checks, no file inventory.
11. **Apply what they say.** Map the plain words to one correction (`references/reading-and-tone.md`, section 12):
    ```
    python "<scripts>\curation_tools.py" correct --folder "<listing>" --find "<words>"
    python "<scripts>\curation_tools.py" correct --folder "<listing>" --who "<name as printed>" --set price_reaction=not_raised --why "<their words>"
    python "<scripts>\curation_tools.py" correct --folder "<listing>" --who "<name>" --reword "<old sentence>" --to "<new sentence>"
    ```
    After `--set`, bring every sentence it prints about that person into line. Rebuild with the run line from step 4 minus `--sheet` and `--as-of` (or the NEXT line), replace the same draft's body with `update_draft` (`draftId`, `htmlBody` only), run `draft_check.py` again, and say in one or two plain sentences what changed. If `update_draft` is refused, tell Graeham what to change in the draft himself, and never work around the refusal.
12. **When they say it went out:** `python "<scripts>\snapshot_issue.py" record --folder "<listing>" --issue <N> --sent-on <YYYY-MM-DD>`. The issue folder is then the record of what the seller received, and a rebuild of it needs `--replace-sent` on Graeham's word.

Use `--waive`, `--allow-name`, `--allow-term`, `--brief-file`, `--issue` and `--replace-sent` only when Graeham says so. Do not pass `--no-pdf` on a rebuild: the older PDF would stay. The link copy is published, and the PDF attached or filed, only on his word (`references/delivery.md`).

## Known limits (say so, do not paper over)

- The Gmail connector strips images, call links and background colors, so the draft has no photo and the phone is plain text. The photo shows in the link copy and the PDF.
- No script reads Glide or Supra emails, pasted notes or forwarded emails: they are entered by hand (`references/intake.md`).
- One workbook per run. The live sheet and an attached copy are not merged.
- What a date in the sheet's Email, Call and Text columns means is still open: Graeham reads it as the day they called or the day it was entered, and it is to be confirmed with Adrian (`references/counting-rules.md`, section 5; as of Oct 13, 2026 the email asking him had not gone out, and no session sends it without Graeham's word). Keep the current reading. The build does not work out the other reading, so when a readings file turned one of those dates into a reply, put both totals in a glance line.
- A listing with a combination lockbox has no electronic showing record: `tracking.keybox_showings` false with a `tracking.lockbox` note, and showings are the tours agents reported.
- The Gmail-safe copy's split website has not yet been through the connector: the two drafts of Oct 13, 2026 were made from an earlier hand-made copy and read back with the website as a google.com redirect link. It is proven once a draft made from `email_gmail.html` passes `draft_check.py`.
- The hand-written reports counted cancelled tours, questions and open house hosts; this one does not, so its totals run lower. Be ready to say so when asked.
- The MLS grid script has been tested on synthetic pages only. A heading it does not know may need an alias after the first live run, and an M.O. flag drawn as an icon with no words is refused rather than read (`references/extras.md`, section 3).
