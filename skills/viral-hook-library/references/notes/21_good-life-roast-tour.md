## HOOK (first 1-3s)
Suited agent walks straight at camera down a suburban driveway, sky ultra-wide-angle dramatic, and ROASTS the viewer: giant white caps captions "DOES YOUR LIFE" → "ACTUALLY SUCK?" Negative-hook question aimed at the viewer's own life, not the house — pure pattern interrupt for a listing video.

## STRUCTURE
- 0-6s: The roast: "Does your life actually suck? ... hanging around broke partners?" — walking shots, captions "DOES YOUR LIFE / ACTUALLY SUCK? / BROKE PARTNERS? / HANGING AROUND".
- 7-12s: "DOES IT FEEL LIKE..." — continues across street + symmetric driveway wide.
- 13-15s: Comedy beat: basketball bounces past him mid-line, "IT'S NOT YOU" (it's your zip code).
- 16-21s: Flip to the pitch: wooden footbridge at the golf club — "THIS IS UPLANDS"; horse-club fence line "HOPE TO WIN".
- 22-24s: Drone aerial: enclave + Toronto skyline on horizon (neighborhood flex).
- 25-30s: Lifestyle montage: strolling past a mansion "OUT WITH RICH KIDS"; sitting on a playground slide in a suit "AT THE PARK".
- 31-33s: Stepping out of a luxury car at iron gates "TO MAKE RICH FRIENDS".
- 34-40s: Punchline: blazer + swim trunks, feet in the pool, arms wide — "A NEW MINDSET ... BOULEVARD" (lands the address: 55 Riverside Blvd).
- 41-42s: White end card with KASH ALAVI logo.

## TEXT OVERLAYS
Karaoke captions: 1-3 words per beat, ALL CAPS, white extra-bold sans with black outline, locked center-screen. No other graphics until logo end card. Words punch in sync with his VO.

## TRANSITIONS & EDIT RHYTHM
Hard cuts only, one location per sentence fragment (~2-3s each) — the jump-cut location chain is the energy source; his walking motion toward camera stitches scenes so cuts feel continuous (soft match on action). One drone insert as the mid-video "wow" breath. Ends on a static card. No effects, no speed ramps — comedy timing over beat-sync.

## AI vs REAL
100% real, one agent + gimbal/phone + drone. The production trick is scripting, not VFX: each location is a joke setup (playground, horses, pool). Zero AI.

## CONCEPT / WHY IT WORKS
Negative hook + lifestyle-not-house selling: he insults the viewer's current life ("it's not you, it's where you live"), then presents the neighborhood as the fix — classic PAS (problem-agitate-solve) compressed into 42s of deadpan. Selling the enclave (golf, horses, parks, rich neighbors) makes the actual house the afterthought CTA, which is what luxury buyers actually buy.
