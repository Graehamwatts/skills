## CONCEPT / WHY IT WORKS
Meme-fluent listing tour: price shock is immediately defused with a laughing meme, converting a sales pitch into an in-joke the viewer shares with the brokerage (parasocial "we're laughing together" effect). Personality props (chalk caricature, camera-op teasing the agent) make a boutique brand feel like a sitcom cast — retention comes from waiting for the next gag, not the next room.
