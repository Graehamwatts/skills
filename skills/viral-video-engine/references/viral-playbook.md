# The Watts Viral Video Playbook
### Research synthesis — July 2026. Source of truth for all listing/media video content.

## 1. Why this works (the mechanics)

The first 3 seconds decide everything. Completion rate is the primary algorithmic signal on IG/TikTok — a 25s video watched to the end beats a 60s video abandoned at 40%. Listings with video get ~403% more inquiries than those without. Reels get ~2x the reach of static posts and ~22% more engagement than carousels.

**The three levers, in order of impact: hook (0–3s) → retention architecture (cuts, loops, captions) → CTA.**

## 2. Hook formulas (proven patterns, ranked)

1. **Price anchor / "What $X buys"** — "This is what $924,000 buys on the Peninsula right now." Leads with a number; numbers stop scrolls. Works because everyone has an opinion on home prices.
2. **Price-guess engagement bait** — "How much do you think this house costs?" + reveal at the end. Drives comments (comments > likes for reach). Ask for the guess BEFORE the reveal.
3. **Contrarian/negation** — "The house isn't the story here. The land is." / "Everyone says X. This one says otherwise."
4. **Flaw-forward honesty** — "This $924K listing has one problem nobody mentions..." Honesty reads as trust; the flaw is the open loop.
5. **Story cold-open (Glennda Baker pattern)** — start mid-story with the idea up front: "My seller cried at closing. Here's why." No intro, no logo, no name until ~20s in.
6. **Local specificity** — name the street, the employer, the commute. "Ten minutes from Stanford" beats "great location" every time.

**Hook rules:** no greetings, no "hey guys," no logos first. The hook is a promise — the video must pay it off. Pair a verbal pattern-interrupt with a visual one (walking shot, drone reveal, unexpected first frame).

## 3. Retention architecture

**Short-form (Reels/TikTok/Shorts):**
- Sweet spot 15–30s for pure listing reels; up to 60s only if every second earns it.
- Front-load cuts: ~40% of all cuts in the first 10% of runtime (Vaibhav arc — already our locked system). New visual every 1.5–2.5s in the body.
- Burned-in animated captions: 3–5 words per line, max 2 lines, keyword highlights in brand color. Captioned videos hold viewers ~53% longer; most watch muted.
- One open loop minimum: tease the payoff ("wait till you see the lot") before the midpoint.
- End loops cleanly — a strong last frame improves replays, and replays count.

**Long-form (YouTube):**
- Kill the cinematic intro. Hook 0–5s (direct, local, specific), setup 5–15s listing exactly what's coming, then deliver.
- Start tours INSIDE the story, not at the mailbox. Open loops between sections ("the most valuable part isn't inside the house").
- Visual reset every ~20 seconds: new b-roll, new graphic, new angle. This is the single biggest retention lever in long-form.
- Chapters on, progress markers, mini-payoffs per section, one emotional stake.
- Retention is architecture, not decoration — built into structure before the edit.

## 4. Edit grammar (the locked Watts system)

- **Composition:** the 5 Vaibhav modes (hook composite, full-bleed talking head, full-bleed b-roll, screenshot PiP, section header). One avatar look per video, on-location (front-of-house / in-home), never desk, never digital twin.
- **Footage hierarchy:** real 4K listing footage first, Seedance/Higgsfield AI b-roll only for moves that don't exist (aerial rises, impossible pushes), photo zoompan as last resort.
- **Cuts timed to words:** cut on sentence boundaries from word-level timestamps (Whisper), not on gut feel.
- **Typography:** Playfair Display Italic (subjects/emotion), DM Sans (stats/labels, letter-spaced), Inter (captions), gold #E3C567 highlights on black pills — brand-locked.
- **Grade:** warm subject / cool background. Gold section washes.
- **Audio:** VO at full level; music bed at −18 to −20 dB (0.08–0.12 gain), fade out last 2s. Licensed catalog or generated only — never trending audio we don't have rights to on business accounts.

## 5. Resolution & export (quality without wasted pixels)

| Platform | Deliver | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | 1080×1920, H.264, 8–10 Mbps, 30fps, AAC 256k/48kHz | IG re-encodes everything to ~1080p; a clean high-bitrate 1080p master survives compression better than bloated files. Turn ON "High-Quality Uploads" in app settings. Export from 4K timeline. |
| TikTok | 1080×1920, H.264, ~10 Mbps | Same logic; TikTok caps playback ~1080p. |
| YouTube Shorts | 1080×1920 (or 2160×3840 if the master allows) | Shorts accepts 4K; higher upload res gets a better codec tier. |
| YouTube long-form | 3840×2160 4K master upload whenever sources allow | 4K uploads get YouTube's higher-quality codec tier, which makes even 1080p playback visibly sharper. Raw listing footage is already 4K — cut long-form on a 4K timeline. |

**Master rule: edit on a 4K timeline (sources are 4K), export a 4K archive master, derive platform files from it.** AI b-roll (720p native) gets upscaled (Higgsfield `upscale_video` to 2K/4K) before the 4K timeline.

## 6. CTA system (DM / call primary)

High-intent, low-friction, spoken AND on-screen — one CTA per video:

- **Listing videos:** "DM me 'BRADLEY' for the full details and private tour times — or call me directly, number's in my bio."
- **Price-guess format:** "DM me your guess and I'll tell you if you're right — and what it'd take to get the keys."
- **Market updates:** "DM me 'MARKET' and I'll send you the full report for your zip code."
- **YouTube:** verbal CTA + pinned comment + description first-2-lines with phone number and DM instruction.

Rules: say it, show it (text overlay in the last 3–5s), pin it (comment). DM keywords stay compatible with GHL comment-keyword automation (EPA, SELL, BUY...) — comment keyword for automation-scale, DM/call for high-intent capture. **Speed-to-lead: reply inside 5 minutes = ~100x more likely to connect than 30 minutes.** Every video CTA presumes the follow-up system is on.

## 7. Formats that earn views (the rotation)

1. What $X buys in [city] (series — the franchise)
2. Price-guess reveal tour
3. Silent cinematic teaser (music + text only)
4. The honest tour (flaw-forward)
5. Story-driven deal recap (Glennda pattern)
6. Lot/land value angle ("the dirt is the story")
7. Long-form YouTube tour with the numbers section

Batch production (Glennda model): shoot/generate a month of avatar segments in one session, post daily in the 6:30–9:30am window.
